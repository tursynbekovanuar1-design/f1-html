    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is a Constructor's Championship?

    The World Constructors' Championship (WCC) is awarded by the FIA to the most successful F1 constructor over the course of the season, based on points accumulated by both team drivers.

    How are race points allocated?

    Points are awarded to the top 10 finishing drivers at each Grand Prix: 25, 18, 15, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 2, 1. An extra point is awarded for the fastest lap if finished in top 10.

    What are Power Unit regulations?

    F1 cars use 1.6-liter V6 turbocharged hybrid power units with energy recovery systems (ERS) that harvest heat and kinetic energy to boost horsepower.
